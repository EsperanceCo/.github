# Esperance

*Espérance* — French for hope. We build software that gives accountants back the hours reconciliation eats every month.

## What we make

**[Recwise](https://github.com/EsperanceCo/recwise)** — offline bank reconciliation for accountants. Import a bank statement and a ledger, match them with explainable rules, review anything uncertain, and get a reconciliation statement out the other end.

- **Local-first** — no network calls, no telemetry, no accounts. Your data stays on your machine.
- **Explainable** — every match records its tier, a confidence score, and a plain-English reason.
- **Human in the loop** — nothing posts automatically; uncertain matches go to review.
- **Exact money** — decimal arithmetic only, never floats.
